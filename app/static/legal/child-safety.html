LikeMinded - Child Safety Standards

Last updated: April 3, 2026

LikeMinded is committed to the safety and protection of minors. Our platform is designed exclusively for adults aged 18 and older. We take child safety seriously and have implemented the following standards to prevent child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform.

1. Age Restriction

  • LikeMinded is strictly for users aged 18 and older.
  • Age verification is enforced during onboarding — users must confirm they are at least 18 years old.
  • Users who are found to be under 18 will have their accounts immediately removed.

2. Identity Verification

  • LikeMinded offers identity verification (KYC) requiring users to submit a government-issued ID and a selfie.
  • This helps ensure users are who they claim to be and meet the minimum age requirement.

3. Zero Tolerance for CSAE Content

  • LikeMinded has a zero-tolerance policy for any content depicting, promoting, or facilitating child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Any such content will be immediately removed and the account permanently banned.
  • We will report all instances of CSAE content to the relevant law enforcement authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable.

4. Reporting Mechanisms

  • Users can report any concerning behavior or content directly within the app using the in-app reporting feature.
  • Reports can be submitted during any conversation by selecting "Report User" from the conversation menu.
  • Report categories include harassment, scam, hate, sexual content, impersonation, spam, and other.
  • All reports are reviewed by our moderation team.

5. Content Moderation

  • Our team reviews reports and takes action including warnings, conversation termination, and permanent account bans.
  • Photos shared on the platform are subject to review.
  • Chat messages can be reported and reviewed for policy violations.

6. Photo Sharing Safeguards

  • Photos between matched users are not shared immediately — they require a 24-hour waiting period and mutual consent from both users.
  • This safeguard helps prevent unwanted or inappropriate photo sharing.

7. Cooperation with Authorities

  • LikeMinded cooperates fully with law enforcement investigations related to child safety.
  • We comply with all applicable child safety laws and report to regional and national authorities as required.
